# Experiments: evidence pages

These pages hold the long record behind the experiments chapter: the numbered sources, the dated readings of the archive, the plans and the stage-by-stage results. They were the bulk of three pages that had grown to about 98,000 words (counted on 20261104 before the split); on 20261104 the three pages were cut down to short result pages and the rest was moved here without changing a word, so that a reader can see the answer first and the evidence when it is needed.

Each experiment page has its own sources page: a number in brackets on a result page or on an evidence page refers to the list on the sources page of the same experiment, and the numbers did not change when the pages were split.
