# FB15k-237

This page answers what the FB15k-237 data contains, how it was cut from [Freebase](freebase.md), and what its licence status is. It is a set of about 310 thousand [triples](../index/triple.md) used to test [link prediction](../index/link-prediction.md). It replaced FB15k, whose test set leaked answers. How systems are scored on it, and the results, are on [the link-prediction benchmark page](../benchmarks/kg-link-prediction.md).

## What it contains

Each line is a triple: a head entity, a relation and a tail entity, tab-separated. Entities are Freebase machine identifiers such as `/m/08966` [4]. There are 237 relation types [1][4]. In the copy counted for this page, 144 of the 237 relation names are dotted paths, such as `/award/award_nominee/award_nominations./award/award_nomination/award`. They cover 184,833 of the 272,115 training triples (68%) [4].

These paths come from how Freebase stored n-ary facts: an intermediate "compound value type" node joined the parts, and the binary relations were made by walking through that node [7].

The official release also carries textual mentions of entity pairs, from about 200 million ClueWeb12 sentences with FACC1 annotations [2]. The ConvE repository's copy holds the triples only [3][4].

## Size and versions

| Split | Triples |
|---|---|
| Train | 272,115 |
| Validation | 17,535 |
| Test | 20,466 |
| Total | 310,116 |

There are 14,541 entities and 237 relations, counted from the files [4]. The original paper's dataset table gives the same split sizes [1]. Thirty-seven validation or test triples (9 and 28) have an entity that never occurs in training [4].

The Microsoft Download Center lists one release, "Version: 1.0", file `FB15K-237.2.zip`, 139.4 MB. The page shows two dates: "Last published: October 30, 2015" and "Date Published: 7/15/2024" [2]. The 2015 paper called the set "FB15KSelected" and said its release "will be available soon" [1].

Its parent, FB15k, has 14,951 entities, 1,345 relations and 592,213 triples (483,142 / 50,000 / 59,071) [6].

## Licence

**Unstated.** The download page gives no licence, only a generic link to Microsoft's terms of use [2]. The README inside the 139.4 MB zip was not read. The ConvE repository, which redistributes the triples, has an MIT `LICENSE` file; whether it covers the data files is unstated [3].

For context only: Google distributes the Freebase dumps under CC BY and its terms of service [10]. Whether that carries over to this subset is unstated.

## How it was made

Toutanova and Chen built it from FB15k in four steps [1]:

1. Keep the 401 most frequent relations of FB15k.
2. Detect near-duplicate and inverse relations: two relations are merged when at least 97% of their entity pairs coincide, or of their reversed pairs.
3. Keep one relation of each such set. That leaves 237.
4. Drop validation and test triples whose entity pair is directly linked in the training data.

The reason was test leakage. In FB15k, 80.9% of test triples had their entity pair linked in training; in WN18 it was 94.0%; in the new set it is 0 by construction [1]. FB15k itself was built by Bordes et al. from Freebase entities that are also in the Wikilinks database and have at least 100 mentions in Freebase. They removed explicit reverse relations such as `!/people/person/nationality`, then split the triples at random [6].

## Known problems

- **Fixed-set relations.** About 12.7% of test queries use relations that link entities to a fixed set of values, such as the 12 months of a climate relation. They are trivial after filtering out known tails [7].
- **Skew.** In 15.98% of test triples the relation is skewed 50% or more toward one head or tail entity [7]. The CoDEx authors read this as a reason why a baseline that only counts frequencies does well on it [7]; its score is on the [benchmark page](../benchmarks/kg-link-prediction.md).
- **Symmetric relations survive.** The filtering does not remove all symmetric relations such as "similar to" [5].
- **Unseen entities.** The 37 triples above cannot be predicted by methods that need a trained embedding per entity [4].
- **Evaluation.** Published scores changed when ties were broken at random; see the [benchmark page](../benchmarks/kg-link-prediction.md) [8].
- **Source retired.** CoDEx calls Freebase deprecated in favour of Wikidata [7]. The date of the Freebase snapshot behind FB15k is unstated in the sources read.
